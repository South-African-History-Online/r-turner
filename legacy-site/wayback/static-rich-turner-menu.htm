On 8 January 1978 Richard Turner was shot through a window of his suburban Durban home and died. January 8, 2008 marks the 30th anniversary of his assassination. To commemorate the assassination of this remarkable South African political activist and philosopher, SAHO and the Turner family have compiled a special website about his life and and his writings.

This quote from Martin Luther King was read by Jann and Kim at Rich Turner’s funeral:

"If a man happens to be 36-years-old, as I happen to be, and some great truth stands before the door of his life--some great opportunity to stand up for that which is right and that which is true, A man might be afraid his home will get bombed, or he's afraid that he will lose his job, or he's afraid that he will get shot, and he may go on and live until he's 80. The cessation of breathing in his life is merely the belated announcement of an earlier death of the spirit.

A man dies when he refuses to stand up for that which is right. A man dies when he refuses to stand up for justice. A man dies when he refuses to take a stand for that which is true. So we are going to stand right here letting the world know that we are determined to be free."
